Features

Everything you need,
nothing you don't

Built for communities that care about privacy, performance, and control.

💬
Real-time Messaging
Instant messages with threaded conversations, reactions, edits, and deletes. WebSocket-powered for sub-100ms delivery.
🎙️
Voice & Video
Crystal-clear voice channels powered by WebRTC. Video calls and screen sharing on the roadmap.
🔐
Privacy First
No trackers. No ads. No data harvesting. End-to-end encryption in development. Your conversations stay yours.
🌐
Self-Hostable
Deploy on your own infrastructure in minutes. Full control over your data, backups, and uptime.
🤖
Bot Ecosystem
Rich plugin and bot framework. Build custom automation, moderation tools, and integrations with any service.
🎨
Custom Themes
Full UI personalization. Custom CSS support, dark/light modes, and community-made themes.
